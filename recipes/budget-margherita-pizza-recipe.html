Budget Margherita Pizza Recipe ($2 Per Person)

Pizza night does not have to cost $25 a head. A tin of tomatoes, one ball of mozzarella and a fast 30-minute dough make two proper margherita pizzas for about $8 - roughly $2 per person and far cheaper than delivery.

30 minutes $2.00 per serving 2 pizzas Vegetarian 4.8 from 95 cooks

Why This Recipe Is So Cheap

  • Flour, yeast and salt cost about $0.62 for both bases.
  • Tinned San Marzano style tomatoes beat expensive fresh ones for flavour and price.
  • Half a ball of mozzarella per pizza is plenty when you stretch it with grated parmesan.

Ingredients (makes 4 servings)

Prices are typical New York supermarket prices in September 2026 and come to about $8.00 in total - that is $2.00 per serving.

  • 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour - $0.28
  • 1 tsp instant yeast - $0.07
  • 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp salt - $0.05
  • 2 tbsp olive oil - $0.22
  • 150 ml warm water
  • 1 tin (400 g) crushed tomatoes - $1.19
  • 1 clove garlic, grated - $0.10
  • 125 g fresh mozzarella, torn - $1.99
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan - $0.55
  • Handful of fresh basil - $1.20
  • Dried oregano and chilli flakes - $0.05

How to Make It, Step by Step

  1. Stir the flour, yeast, sugar and salt together, add the olive oil and warm water, then knead for 5 minutes until smooth.
  2. Cover the dough and let it rest while the oven heats to its highest setting (250C / 480F) with a tray or pizza stone inside.
  3. Warm the crushed tomatoes with the grated garlic, a pinch of salt and the oregano - do not cook them down, keep the sauce bright.
  4. Split the dough into 2 balls and stretch each one on a floured sheet of baking paper to about 25 cm wide.
  5. Spread 3-4 tbsp of sauce per base, leaving a 2 cm border, then scatter over the mozzarella and parmesan.
  6. Bake 6-7 minutes each until the crust is spotted and the cheese bubbles.
  7. Finish with torn basil, a drizzle of olive oil and chilli flakes, then slice and serve straight away.

Chef Tips That Keep the Cost Down

  • Bake on the lowest shelf for a crisp base, then give it 1 minute under the grill for colour.
  • Cheap mozzarella releases water - pat it dry with kitchen paper so the middle does not go soggy.
  • No pizza stone? An upturned preheated baking tray works just as well.
  • Freeze the second stretched base on its paper and bake it another night for an instant cheap dinner.
