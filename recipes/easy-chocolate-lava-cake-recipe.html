Easy Chocolate Lava Cake Recipe (Under $1.50 a Serving)

A lava cake looks like a $12 restaurant dessert but costs about $1.45 a portion and takes 17 minutes. All you need is chocolate, butter, eggs, sugar, a little flour and a hot oven - plus the one timing rule that guarantees a molten middle.

Why This Recipe Is So Cheap

  • Six ingredients, all of which you probably already have in the cupboard.
  • No cream, no vanilla pods and no fancy moulds needed - two ramekins are enough.
  • Made in 17 minutes, so no long oven time burning through your energy bill.

Ingredients (makes 2 servings)

Prices are typical New York supermarket prices in September 2026 and come to about $2.90 in total - that is $1.45 per serving.

  • 100 g dark chocolate (55-70% cocoa) - $1.10
  • 60 g butter - $0.55
  • 2 eggs - $0.55
  • 50 g caster sugar - $0.12
  • 25 g plain flour - $0.04
  • Pinch of salt and a little butter and cocoa for dusting - $0.10
  • Optional: ice cream or berries to serve

How to Make It, Step by Step

  1. Heat the oven to 220C / 430F and butter two ramekins, then dust them with cocoa powder so the cakes release cleanly.
  2. Melt the chocolate and butter together in a bowl over barely simmering water, or in short 20-second bursts in the microwave.
  3. Whisk the eggs and sugar for 3 minutes until pale and slightly thickened - this aeration is what gives the crisp top.
  4. Fold the melted chocolate into the eggs, then sift in the flour and salt and fold again until just combined.
  5. Divide between the two ramekins and bake for 11-12 minutes. The tops should be set with a slight wobble in the middle.
  6. Rest for 1 minute, run a knife around the edge, then invert onto plates and serve straight away.

Chef Tips That Keep the Cost Down

  • Set a timer. One minute too long and you have a very nice brownie instead of a lava cake.
  • Make them up to 2 days ahead and bake straight from the fridge, adding 2 minutes to the bake time.
  • Cheap chocolate works fine - just pick one with at least 55% cocoa for a proper molten centre.
  • No ramekins? Use a muffin tin with paper cases and shorten the bake by 1 minute.
