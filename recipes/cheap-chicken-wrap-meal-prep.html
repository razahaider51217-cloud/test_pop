Chicken Wrap Meal Prep Recipe (5 Lunches for $10)

Buying lunch in New York costs $14 a day, or roughly $70 a week. These wraps cost $1.98 each, take 35 minutes on a Sunday and keep for five days in the fridge. They are also the same filling we roll in our Sarab kitchen for the lunch rush.

35 minutes $1.98 per lunch 5 wraps High protein 4.9 from 64 cooks

Why This Recipe Is So Cheap

  • One pack of tortillas and one tray of chicken thighs cover five whole lunches.
  • Greek yogurt instead of mayo makes a cheaper, higher-protein sauce.
  • Fridge-raid vegetables keep the cost down and stop anything going to waste.

Ingredients (makes 5 servings)

Prices are typical New York supermarket prices in September 2026 and come to about $9.90 in total - that is $1.98 per serving.

  • 5 large tortillas - $2.15
  • 500 g chicken thighs or breast - $6.20
  • 4 tbsp Greek yogurt - $0.55
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder - $0.35
  • 1 tbsp olive oil - $0.15
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce - $0.45
  • 1 tomato, diced - $0.55
  • Half a red onion, sliced - $0.30
  • 60 g grated cheddar - $0.85
  • Salt, pepper and chilli flakes - $0.05

How to Make It, Step by Step

  1. Toss the chicken with the olive oil, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook in a hot pan for 12-15 minutes, turning once, until cooked through. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice into strips.
  3. Stir the Greek yogurt with the lemon juice, a pinch of salt and chilli flakes to make a quick cheap sauce.
  4. Warm the tortillas for 15 seconds each so they fold without cracking.
  5. Spread a spoon of yogurt sauce down the middle, then layer chicken, lettuce, tomato, red onion and cheddar.
  6. Fold in the sides and roll tightly, then wrap each one in baking paper or foil so they hold their shape.
  7. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To serve, unwrap and eat cold, or microwave for 60 seconds if you prefer them warm.

Chef Tips That Keep the Cost Down

  • Keep the sauce and wet vegetables away from the tortilla edge or the wrap will go soft by Wednesday.
  • Chicken thighs are cheaper and stay tender when reheated - use them if you can.
  • Double the filling and freeze half for the following week to save another $10.
  • Add a handful of rice or couscous for a heartier wrap at about 30 cents extra per portion.
