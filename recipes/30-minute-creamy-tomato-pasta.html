30-Minute Creamy Tomato Pasta Recipe (Under $2.50 a Serving)

This is the meal we recommend to anyone learning to cook on a budget. Ten pantry ingredients, one pan, 30 minutes and about $2.45 a serving - and it tastes like a restaurant pasta because of one trick: finishing the sauce with starchy pasta water.

30 minutes $2.45 per serving One pan Vegetarian 4.9 from 142 cooks

Why This Recipe Is So Cheap

  • Dried pasta and tinned tomatoes are among the cheapest calories in the store.
  • Cream cheese costs a fraction of heavy cream and gives the same silky finish.
  • No meat needed - parmesan and a little garlic carry all the flavour.

Ingredients (makes 4 servings)

Prices are typical New York supermarket prices in September 2026 and come to about $9.80 in total - that is $2.45 per serving.

  • 400 g dried pasta (penne, rigatoni or fusilli) - $1.15
  • 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes - $1.19
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste - $0.20
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced - $0.15
  • 1 small onion, finely diced - $0.60
  • 3 tbsp cream cheese - $0.55
  • 40 g grated parmesan - $0.95
  • 2 tbsp olive oil - $0.22
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp chilli flakes - $0.10
  • Fresh basil and salt to finish - $0.70
  • 4.5 litres of water for boiling (free)

How to Make It, Step by Step

  1. Boil the pasta in well salted water for 1 minute less than the pack says. Reserve a full mug of the cooking water before draining.
  2. Meanwhile heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat and soften the onion for 5 minutes without colouring it.
  3. Add the garlic and chilli flakes and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant - do not let the garlic brown or it turns bitter.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, cook for 1 minute, then add the chopped tomatoes, oregano and a pinch of salt. Simmer for 12 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream cheese until the sauce is smooth and glossy, then add 3-4 tbsp of the reserved pasta water.
  6. Tip the drained pasta into the sauce and toss for 1 minute over the heat so every piece is coated and the sauce clings.
  7. Finish off the heat with the parmesan, torn basil, a drizzle of olive oil and extra chilli flakes if you like it hot.

Chef Tips That Keep the Cost Down

  • Never rinse pasta. The starch on the surface is what thickens the sauce and makes it stick.
  • The sauce should be a little looser than you want at the end - it thickens fast as you toss.
  • Add a drained tin of tuna, chickpeas or leftover roast chicken for protein at about 60 cents a portion.
  • Freeze leftover sauce in ice-cube trays and you always have a cheap dinner base ready.
