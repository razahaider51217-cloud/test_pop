Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. Overview

Sarab Fast Food & Restaurant ("Sarab", "we", "us") operates sarabfood.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you browse our menu, read our cheap recipes and cooking guides, place an order, book a table or contact us, and how we use, store and protect that information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: name, email address, phone number, delivery address, reservation details and message content submitted through our contact, reservation or newsletter forms.
  • Order information: items ordered, order value, delivery preferences and payment status (card data is processed by our payment provider - we never store full card numbers).
  • Automatically collected data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, referring URL and approximate location derived from your IP address.

3. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies and local storage to keep the site working, remember your cookie choice and measure how our pages perform. Cookies fall into these categories:

  • Essential cookies: required for the site to load and for forms to work. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4 helps us understand which menu items and cheap recipe guides are most useful.
  • Advertising cookies: Google Ads cookies let us measure ad campaigns and show relevant offers to people who have visited our site.

On your first visit you will see a consent banner. You can accept all cookies or allow essential cookies only, and you can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser storage for this site.

4. Google Analytics & Google Ads

This website uses Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads conversion tracking. Google may set cookies and receive your IP address when these services run. We use Google Consent Mode v2, meaning advertising and analytics storage stay disabled until you give consent. You can review Google's practices at How Google uses information from sites that use our services and opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare, deliver and support your orders and reservations.
  • To reply to enquiries, catering requests and feedback.
  • To send offers and recipe updates you have subscribed to (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and website performance.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate: payment processors, delivery partners, email and hosting providers, and advertising/analytics providers such as Google - each bound by their own contractual and legal obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal information, to object to processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell it). California residents may exercise CCPA/CPRA rights, and visitors in the EEA/UK may exercise GDPR rights. Email privacy@sarabfood.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention & Security

Order and reservation records are kept for as long as needed for tax, accounting and dispute purposes. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and staff training to protect your data, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our pages may link to third-party sites such as delivery platforms or review services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above always shows the current version.
