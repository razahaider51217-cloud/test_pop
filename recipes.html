How We Keep Every Recipe Cheap

  • Costed per serving, not per dish. Every recipe page lists an itemised cost breakdown so you can see exactly where the money goes.
  • Pantry-first ingredients. Flour, tinned tomatoes, pasta and spices carry most of these recipes and keep the price under $4 a portion.
  • Cheaper cuts, better results. Chicken thighs, 80/20 beef and tinned tomatoes beat their expensive equivalents on flavour as well as price.
  • No waste. Leftover sauce, dough and fillings are frozen or reused, so nothing you buy ends up in the bin.
  • 35 minutes or less. Fast recipes mean less energy used and a much better chance you cook instead of ordering in.
